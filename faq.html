How do Water ATM machines work?

Our Water ATM machines function as automated water dispensing systems. Here's how they operate:

  • Water is sourced from your connection and passes through our multi-stage purification system
  • Customers make payment through M-Pesa, smart cards, or coins depending on your chosen model
  • The system verifies payment and dispenses the exact amount of water purchased
  • Our machines include real-time monitoring systems allowing you to track sales, water levels, and machine status remotely
  • Each machine includes UV sterilization as the final purification step before dispensing to ensure water safety
What are the power and water requirements?

Power requirements:

  • Standard models: 220-240V AC connection
  • Solar-powered options available for areas with unreliable electricity
  • Power consumption: 0.5-1.5 kWh per day depending on usage
  • Built-in power stabilization to protect against power fluctuations
  • Optional backup battery system ensures operation during outages

Water requirements:

  • Connection to municipal water supply or borehole/well
  • Minimum water pressure: 1 bar
  • Storage tank capacity: 500-2000 liters (customizable)
  • Daily capacity: Up to 5000 liters depending on model
What payment options are available for customers?

Our Water ATMs support multiple payment solutions to maximize convenience:

  • Mobile Money Integration: Direct M-Pesa, Airtel Money and other mobile payment integration
  • RFID/Smart Cards: Reloadable cards that customers can top up for regular use
  • Coin Acceptance: Traditional coin operation available on select models
  • QR Code Payments: Scan-and-pay functionality via smartphone
  • Subscription Model: Monthly subscription options for regular customers

All payment transactions are securely recorded in our cloud-based management system, allowing you to track sales in real-time and generate comprehensive reports.

What safety and hygiene features are included?

Our Water ATMs prioritize water safety and hygiene with these features:

  • Multi-stage purification:
    • Sediment filtration (5-10 micron)
    • Activated carbon filtration
    • Ultrafiltration membrane (0.01 micron)
    • Reverse osmosis (where required)
    • UV sterilization as final safety step
  • Automatic sanitization: Scheduled UV sanitization of dispensing nozzles
  • Quality monitoring: Real-time water quality sensors with alerts
  • Anti-contamination design: Protected dispensing area to prevent external contamination
  • Automatic shutdown: If water quality parameters fall below set thresholds
  • Regular testing: Maintenance packages include regular water quality testing

All our systems comply with Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS) requirements for drinking water quality.

What licenses or permits are required?

Operating a Water ATM business in Kenya typically requires:

  • Water vending permit from the local county government
  • Water quality certification from KEBS
  • Business permit from your county government
  • Public health certificate for the premises
  • If bottling water: KEBS standardization mark

Our consultants provide guidance through the licensing process and can connect you with experts who specialize in obtaining these permits. We also provide all necessary technical documentation required for the application process.
