About Johntech Solutions

Pioneering water purification technology since 2010, we're committed to providing businesses with innovative, sustainable, and cost-effective water solutions.

Our Mission

At Johntech Solutions, our mission is to revolutionize water access and quality for businesses worldwide. We believe that clean, safe water should be accessible to everyone, and our innovative water vending machines and purification systems make this possible while creating valuable revenue streams for our clients.


We strive to reduce plastic waste by providing alternatives to bottled water, and our advanced filtration technologies ensure that every drop dispensed meets the highest quality standards. By combining cutting-edge technology with sustainable practices, we're helping businesses across industries meet their water needs while contributing to a healthier planet.
