Mary Wanjiku's Water Empire
From small kiosk to multiple water stations

Mary started with a single Johntech Water ATM in a busy Nairobi suburb in 2023. Within her first month, she was serving over 100 customers daily. The reliable technology and minimal maintenance requirements allowed her to focus on marketing and customer service.

By the end of her first year, Mary had invested in two additional water stations in neighboring communities. Her business now serves over 1,000 families with clean, affordable drinking water.

"What impressed me most was how quickly I started seeing returns," says Mary. "The automatic payment system means I can run this business alongside my other ventures without being physically present all day."

3 Water ATMs
KSh 180,000 monthly revenue
John Mwangi's Community Solution
Bringing clean water to rural communities

In rural Nyeri County, access to clean drinking water was a major challenge. John Mwangi recognized this as both a business opportunity and a chance to make a real difference in his community.

With financing assistance from Johntech Solutions, John installed a high-capacity Water ATM with solar power backup. The machine now serves over 200 households daily, eliminating the need for long walks to collect water from questionable sources.

"The technical support from Johntech has been invaluable," John explains. "Whenever there's an issue, their team responds quickly, ensuring my business continues running smoothly."

Aisha Omar's School Initiative
From educator to water entrepreneur

As a school administrator in Mombasa, Aisha Omar was concerned about students' access to clean drinking water. She approached Johntech with a unique proposal: installing a Water ATM that would serve both the school and surrounding community.

Today, her Water ATM provides subsidized water to students while offering competitive rates to community members. The business generates enough profit to cover maintenance costs and provide additional income for school programs.

"The flexible payment options and customizable pricing have been crucial to our success," says Aisha. "Students use prepaid cards while community members can pay via mobile money."
